Home — Your Day at a Glance

Home is the first thing you see when you open Planita. It brings together your upcoming Events and the things you still need to do, in one simple timeline, so you don't have to check Calendar and Lists separately.

What Home shows

Home lists two kinds of things together, grouped by date: your Calendar Events, and any Reminder in your Lists that has a date and hasn't been completed yet. Reminders without a date, and anything already completed, don't appear here — Home brings together upcoming Events and outstanding dated Reminders, including those that are overdue. Recipes, Watchlist and Books never appear in Home.

Finding your way around Home

Tap the Planita heading at the top of the screen to open the Navigation Wheel, then tap any of the six segments — Home, Calendar, Lists, Recipes, Watchlist, Books — to switch feature. Tapping the segment you're already on simply closes the wheel. The ☰ (menu) button on the top left opens Settings, separately from the wheel.

Using Filter

If you belong to a Group, a Filter button appears near the top of Home. It offers:

  • All Mine (the default) — your own Personal Events and Reminders, plus Group Events you're attending and Group Reminders assigned to you.
  • Personal Only — only your private items.
  • All Group — every Event and Reminder belonging to your Group.
  • Selected Members — pick one or more Group Members to see Events they're attending and Reminders assigned to them; tap Close once you've chosen.

Reset to All Mine returns you to the default view. Filter only appears once you're an active member of a Group — Solo users won't see it.

Opening and editing an item from Home

Tap any row to see its details. For an Event, this opens the same Event view used in Calendar. For a Reminder, it opens a short summary — the Reminder text, its due date, any notes, which List it belongs to, and who it's assigned to.

If you're allowed to change the item, an Edit button appears (both on the detail screen and as a left swipe on the row itself). You can't mark a Reminder as complete from Home — open its List in Lists to do that.

Overdue and upcoming colours

Reminders that are now in the past and still incomplete show a soft red background and the word Overdue. Anything due today through the next three days shows a soft orange background. Everything further ahead has no special colour. Events are never shown as overdue — once an Event has fully finished, it simply drops off Home.

Seeing further ahead

Home initially looks three months ahead. Tap Show More at the bottom of the list to look a further three months ahead, and tap it again for more.

Personal and Group items in Home

Each row shows a small Personal or Group icon and who's involved — for example "You", a list of names, or "Everyone" / "All" for a Group item that hasn't restricted its attendees or assignment. This is for information only; it doesn't change what you can do with the item.

Things Home doesn't do

Home has no Add button of its own — to create a new Event, Reminder, Recipe, Watchlist item or Book, go to that feature and use its own + button. Home also has no search box.
