Privacy Policy

1. What Planita does

Planita helps you organise events, reminders, lists, recipes, films and television, and books. It uses on-device storage and Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services to synchronise information and support optional Group sharing. The inspected app has no custom backend, advertising or third-party analytics or tracking integration.

2. Information you add

Depending on the features you use, Planita stores titles, notes, dates, locations you type, tasks and completion history, recipe text and photographs, viewing and reading progress, ratings, selected images, and other content you choose to enter. This content may identify you or other people.

Planita also stores Group names, Member display names and CloudKit identifiers, participant history and information needed for invitations and sharing. Settings and preferences are stored on your device. The Home screen presents existing Events and Reminders; it does not create a separate Home record.

3. Where information goes

Planita keeps local copies of app data on your device. Personal information synchronises through your private iCloud data. For a Group, the Owner’s Group data is shared with accepted Members through CloudKit. Apple operates the iCloud and CloudKit services under its own terms and privacy information.

Planita uses local notifications for eligible Events and Reminders when you allow them. Notification text can include item titles and a Group name. Planita does not send this content to a separate push server.

4. What Group Members can see

Accepted Members can see information in the Group collection, including existing Group records and Member names or historical references. Calendar events and Lists can be Personal or Group. In an active Group, Recipes, Watchlist and Books do not offer a per-record Personal choice; their Group items are visible to all Members. Joining a Group does not expose your separate Personal Calendar or Lists records.

Removing a Member revokes Group share access but does not remove their contributions or historical associations. Previously downloaded information, screenshots, backups or offline copies may remain outside Planita’s control.

5. Diagnostics and support

Planita keeps a diagnostic log on the device. It can contain device and app details, technical identifiers and information about records. It is not automatically uploaded to us. You may choose to copy and send it, or send screenshots or other details, by email if it will help us investigate a support enquiry.

Support messages can be sent to paul.cozens.apps@icloud.com. Support correspondence and any information you voluntarily provide will be accessible only to the developer and used to investigate and respond to your enquiry. Support correspondence will normally be deleted within 12 months of the enquiry being resolved, unless a longer period is required by law. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated usage statistics and diagnostic information through its App Store services, subject to Apple’s applicable privacy settings.

6. Deletion and choices

You can delete individual items using the controls available in each feature, subject to Group permissions. Synchronisation and deletion across devices can take time. Some recurring item actions retain history needed for recurrence. Planita does not currently offer one “delete all Planita data” control. Removing a Group Member does not delete their Group contributions. Uninstalling the app should not be relied on to delete iCloud data.

If you wish to request the deletion of information, please contact us using the email address above. We will explain the available options and assist where possible. Some information is held within your Apple Account or within Group records managed by another user and may not be directly accessible to the developer.

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information. You can contact us using the email address above to make an enquiry. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

7. Changes

We will update this policy when Planita’s features or data handling change. The effective date and version above will be updated if this policy changes.
