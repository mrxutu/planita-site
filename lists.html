User Guide · Lists

Lists — Stay Organised

Lists is where you keep the things you need to remember and the tasks you need to do — organised into Lists of your own choosing, and shared with your Group where it helps.

Opening Lists

Open the Navigation Wheel and tap Lists. You'll see all your Lists, each showing its icon, name, Personal/Group status, and the number of Reminders still outstanding.

Creating a List

Tap + at the top of Lists. Give it a Title, optional Notes, choose a Colour and Icon for quick recognition, and — if you belong to a Group — choose whether to save it as Personal or to your Group. This choice can't be changed once the List is saved.

Adding a Reminder

Open a List and tap + to add a Reminder. Only a Title is required. You can also set:

  • Priority — None, Low, Medium or High
  • Date, and Time if you want a specific time of day
  • Repeat, if it should recur (see below)
  • Early Reminder — an optional earlier notification, as well as the normal one at the due date/time
  • Assigned to, for Reminders in a Group List
  • Notes

Tap Save.

Completing and reopening a Reminder

Tap the circle to the left of a Reminder to mark it complete; tap it again to reopen it. In a Group List, any Member can complete a shared Reminder — there's one shared tick, not one per person.

Completed Reminders are hidden by default. Open List Options (the ⋯ button) and choose Show Completed to see them again.

Editing, stopping and deleting Reminders

Tap a Reminder's text to edit it. For a Reminder that doesn't repeat, swipe it left to Delete it straightaway. For a repeating Reminder, there's no swipe-to-delete; instead, open its menu (press and hold) and choose Stop Reminder — you'll be asked to confirm, and your completed history is always kept even after stopping.

A Reminder that has already been completed and belongs to a repeating series opens as a read-only record of what was completed, rather than the normal editor.

Repeating Reminders and the 60-day window

Choose Repeat: Never, Every Day, Every Week, Every 2 Weeks, Every Month, Every Year, or a Custom interval of days, weeks, months or years, with an optional end date. Completing the current occurrence reveals the next one; only one occurrence is ever "live" at a time, and your full completion history is kept.

To keep your List focused on what's actually due soon, Planita normally hides upcoming occurrences of a repeating Reminder until they're within 60 days. If you'd like to see further ahead, open List Options and turn on Show future recurring items.

Assigning a Reminder in a Group

In a Group List, you can set Assigned to: None, All (every current Group Member), or specific people. Assignment is just about who a Reminder is relevant to — it doesn't change who can see it, edit it or complete it, and it doesn't create a separate copy for each person.

Reminder priority

Priority (Low, Medium, High) shows as one, two or three exclamation marks next to the Reminder's title, to help you see what matters most at a glance.

List Options

The ⋯ button on a List gives you: Show/Hide Completed, Show future recurring items, Edit List, and Delete List.

Editing and deleting a List

Choose Edit List to change its title, notes, colour or icon (its Personal/Group status can't be changed). Choose Delete List to remove the List and every Reminder inside it — you'll be asked to confirm, as this can't be undone.

Personal and Group Lists

A Personal List is private to you. A Group List is visible to, and can be added to, completed and edited by, every current Group Member.

Things worth knowing

  • There's no standalone To Do feature — all tasks and reminders live inside a List.
  • Lists itself doesn't have a search box.
  • You can reorder Reminders within a List by pressing and dragging them (or using the Move Up/Move Down accessibility actions with VoiceOver).
