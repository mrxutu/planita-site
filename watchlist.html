Watchlist — Keep Track of What You Watch

Watchlist helps you keep track of the films and television programmes you want to watch, are watching, or have finished — on your own or together with your Group.

Opening Watchlist

Open the Navigation Wheel and tap Watchlist. Your list shows every Film or TV title you're currently part of — see "Watching with", below.

Adding a Watchlist item

Tap +. Enter a Title, choose Type (Film or TV), and optionally add a Synopsis and a Poster image. New items default to TV, and start with the status Flagged unless you choose a different starting status.

Status: Flagged, To Watch, Watching, Watched

  • Flagged — you've noticed it, but haven't started watching.
  • To Watch — you're planning to watch it.
  • Watching — you've started.
  • Watched — you've finished.

Once something has genuinely been started or finished, it can't go back to Flagged — but a Watched item can be set back to To Watch if you want to watch it again.

Tracking TV episodes

For a TV item, set the Season and Episode you're up to (starting at Season 1, Episode 1 by default). While you're Watching or it's To Watch, this shows as the next episode to watch; once Watched, it shows the last episode you watched. On the item's detail screen, a quick +1 Episode button lets you move on by exactly one episode without opening the full editor.

Watching with — who's included

Every Watchlist item has a Watching with list of people. Whoever creates the item is always included and can't be removed by anyone else. Anyone else in Watching with can update the shared Status, episode progress and Source, and can add or remove other people (except the creator).

Viewing and editing an item

Tap an item to see its full details, including status, progress, source, who's watching it, and everyone's ratings. If you're part of Watching with, swipe the item left, or use Edit Watchlist Item from its menu, to make changes.

Ratings

Each participant can give a personal rating of 1–5 stars. If someone selects No Longer Watching, their existing rating is retained. Ratings are displayed individually, alongside the Group average, which is simply the average of everyone's ratings, shown to one decimal place once at least one person has rated it. You can change or clear your own rating at any time; nobody else's rating is affected.

Leaving an item, and deleting it

If you're not the creator, choose No Longer Watching from the item's menu to remove yourself from it — it disappears from your own Watchlist, but your rating is kept. The person who created a Watchlist item cannot remove themselves from Watching with. Only the creator can permanently Delete a Watchlist item, which removes it — along with its Poster and everyone's ratings — for everyone in Watching with.

Group Viewing

If you're in a Group, a Group Viewing button at the bottom of Watchlist shows every item any Group Member is currently To Watch, Watching or Watched on — a useful way to see what the household is into, even for items you're not personally part of. Flagged items aren't included here.

When you join a Group, Planita displays the Group collection. Items you created before joining remain in your Personal collection and are not automatically transferred or shared. There is currently no automatic way to move those items into the Group.

Searching and filtering

Use the search box to find an item by title, and Filter to narrow by Status, Type (Film/TV) or Source.

Things worth knowing

  • There's no separate "viewing party" record — Watching with, Status and progress all belong to the one item.
  • Watchlist never appears in Home.
  • Source (for example, which streaming service) is a simple label you manage yourself at Settings → Custom Values → Watchlist Sources.
