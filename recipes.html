Recipes — Your Favourite Food

Recipes keeps your favourite dishes together — ingredients, method, photo and all — ready whenever you need them, and easy to share with your Group.

Opening Recipes

Open the Navigation Wheel and tap Recipes to see your collection. If a Recipe has no photo, Planita shows a simple placeholder image instead.

Adding a Recipe

Tap +. Only a Title is required. You can also add:

  • Chef — who gets credit for the Recipe
  • Description and Source (a web link)
  • A Photo, chosen from your library or taken with the camera
  • Servings, Preparation and Cooking time, in minutes
  • Tags, to help you find it again
  • Ingredients (see below)
  • Method — your preparation steps, as free text

Tap Save.

Adding ingredients

Tap Add Ingredient to add a row, then fill in Quantity (an optional number, such as 1 or 0.5), Unit (choose from a list, or leave blank) and the Ingredient name itself. You can reorder ingredients by dragging, or remove a row with the – button. Manage the list of units you can choose from at Settings → Custom Values → Units — changing that list never rewrites ingredients you've already saved.

Viewing a Recipe

Tap a Recipe to see its full details — photo, description, Chef, servings and times, tags, source, ingredients and method, laid out for easy reading while you cook.

Editing and deleting a Recipe

Only the person who created a Recipe can edit or delete it, even within a Group — this is shown by whether Recipe Options (the ⋯ menu) offers Edit Recipe and Delete Recipe at all. Deleting a Recipe removes it, its ingredients and its photo for everyone in the Group.

Searching and filtering

Use the search box to find a Recipe by title. Tap Filter to narrow the list down by Chef or Tag, and Clear Filters to reset.

Personal and Group Recipes

Recipes doesn't have a Personal/Group choice for each individual Recipe. Instead, your whole Recipes collection follows your overall situation: while you're Solo, your Recipes are private to you; once you're in a Group, new Recipes you create are visible to every Group Member, and so are Recipes other Members create. When you join a Group, Planita displays the Group collection. Items you created before joining remain in your Personal collection and are not automatically transferred or shared. There is currently no automatic way to move those items into the Group.

Things worth knowing

  • "Chef" is just a label of credit — it doesn't give that person any special editing rights.
  • Recipes never appears in Home.
