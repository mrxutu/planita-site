Email support

Contact paul.cozens.apps@icloud.com. Please include a brief description of the issue. Send screenshots or diagnostic logs only when needed, after checking their contents.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move between features?

Tap the Planita heading to open the Navigation Wheel, then choose one of the six features.

Can other Members see my Personal records?

Personal Calendar events and Lists are separate from the Group collection. In an active Group, Recipes, Watchlist and Books work in the shared collection; see Sharing & Groups.

How do I leave a Group?

Ask the Group Owner to remove you. The Owner controls membership. Your contributions and historical associations remain in the Group.

What should I include in a support email?

Describe the feature, what happened and what you expected. Send a screenshot or copied diagnostic log only if needed, after checking that it does not reveal information you would rather keep private.

Does deleting the app erase my iCloud information?

Do not rely on uninstalling Planita to delete iCloud data. Individual records can be deleted through available feature controls. The app does not currently have a single delete-all control.
