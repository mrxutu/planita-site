User Guide · Books

Books — Your Reading Collection

Books helps you keep track of what you're reading, what you've finished, and what you thought of it — with everyone's reading visible together in a Group, while each person's own record stays theirs to manage.

Opening Books

Open the Navigation Wheel and tap Books. If you're in a Group, you'll see every Book every Member has added — each one labelled with who's reading it.

Adding a Book

Tap +. Enter a Title and Author (both required), and optionally:

  • Summary, Source (where you got it from, managed at Settings → Custom Values → Book Sources) and Notes
  • Status — To Read, Reading or Read
  • Date Read
  • Rating — 1 to 5 stars
  • A Cover image, from your library or the camera

Reading status and rating

Set Status to track your progress, and give it a star Rating once you've formed an opinion. These are entirely your own — nobody else's rating or status is affected by yours.

Viewing a Book

Tap a Book to see its cover, title, author, status, date read, rating, summary, source and notes.

Editing and deleting a Book

Each Book belongs to the Reader who added it — they can edit or delete it, shown by whether Book Options offers Edit Book and Delete Book. In a Group, the Group Owner — acting as Group Administrator — can also edit or delete any Group Book, even one added by someone else, without becoming its Reader. Other Members cannot edit or delete another Reader's Book. Different people can add the very same title and author as separate Books; you can't add a duplicate of your own existing Book, but someone else in your Group adding the same title is a separate record of their own reading.

Searching and filtering

Use the search box to find a Book by title or author. Tap Filter to narrow by Status, and — in a Group — by Reader.

Readers in a Group

In a Group, everyone can see everyone's Books, but each Book stays that person's own record. There's no shared "master" copy of a title — if you and someone else in your Group are both reading the same book, you'll each have your own separate Book entry with your own status, notes and rating.

When you join a Group, Planita displays the Group collection. Items you created before joining remain in your Personal collection and are not automatically transferred or shared. There is currently no automatic way to move those items into the Group.

Things worth knowing

  • Books never appears in Home.
  • There's no per-Book Personal/Group choice — your whole Books collection follows whether you're Solo or in a Group, the same way Recipes and Watchlist do.
