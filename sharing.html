Understand your space

Sharing & Groups

A Group lets accepted Members organise shared information together. Check where a record will appear before you add it.

Personal and Group information

Calendar events and Lists have a Personal or Group choice. Personal records are in your private Planita data and are not available to other Group Members through Group sharing. Group records are available to accepted Members, including older records still in the Group.

Different rule for three features

Recipes, Watchlist and Books have no per-record Personal setting in an active Group. Items added to the active Group collection are visible to all Members. Existing Solo items are not automatically moved into the Group.

Joining and managing a Group

The person who creates a Group is its Owner. The Owner sends invitations and manages membership. An invitation grants access only after it is accepted. Planita allows one active Group per person.

The Owner can remove Members. A Member who wants to leave should ask the Owner to remove them. The current app does not provide an Owner departure, transfer or Group closure flow.

What Members can do

  • Calendar and Lists: Members can collaborate on shared records.
  • Recipes: Members can view Group recipes; the creator can edit or delete their own recipe in Planita.
  • Watchlist: Members in “Watching with” can update shared progress and their own ratings. The creator can delete the item.
  • Books: Members can view Group books; the record creator can edit or delete their own book in Planita.

These are Planita app permissions. The underlying CloudKit share has broader access, so these should not be understood as separate server-enforced permissions for every record.

When a Member is removed

Removing a Member revokes their Group share access. Their contributions and historical associations stay in the Group. If the same person is invited again, those associations may become visible to them again. Removal cannot erase information already copied, downloaded, captured in a screenshot or retained on an offline device.

Each person’s Personal records remain separate from the Group. For more detail, see the Privacy Policy.
