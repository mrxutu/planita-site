Sharing & Groups

A Group lets accepted Members organise shared information together. Check where a record will appear before you add it.

Personal and Group information

Calendar Events and Lists have a Personal or Group choice when you create them. Personal records stay private to you — not even the Group Owner can see or administer another Member's Personal records. Group records are available to accepted Members, including older records still in the Group. You cannot move an existing Event or List between Personal and Group after saving it.

Recipes, Watchlist and Books work differently

These collections have no Personal or Group setting for each item. When you join a Group, Planita displays the Group collection. Items you created before joining remain in your Personal collection and are not automatically transferred or shared. There is currently no automatic way to move those items into the Group.

Creating a Group

From Settings → Group, or Create Group if you do not have one yet, give your Group a name and tap Create. You can be part of one active Group at a time, as its Owner or a Member.

Inviting and accepting Members

The Owner opens Settings → Group and taps Invite People. Apple's sharing screen offers Messages, Mail and other available ways to send an invitation. Access begins only after the invitee accepts. To join, open the invitation link and accept it using the options offered on your device. Planita then adds the shared Group, subject to iCloud connectivity.

Group ownership and membership

The person who creates a Group is its Owner. They can rename it, invite people and remove Members. The Owner also acts as the Group's Administrator, with authority to edit or delete any Group record regardless of who created it — see Who can change shared information, below. Planita does not currently offer an Owner departure, ownership transfer or Group closure flow. A Member cannot leave on their own; they can ask the Owner to remove them.

Removing a Member

The Owner opens Settings → Group, finds the person under Members and taps Remove, then confirms. Removal revokes their access to the Group share. Their Personal Events, Lists and Reminders stay separate and unaffected. Content they contributed to the Group remains available to the remaining Members, including Events, Reminders, Recipes, Watchlist items and Books. As Group Administrator, the Owner can edit or delete any of these retained records, the same as any other Group record.

If the same person is invited again and accepts, Planita recognises their previous Group associations, such as assignments, attendance and authorship. Removal cannot erase information already copied, downloaded, captured in a screenshot or retained on an offline device. See the Privacy Policy for more detail.

Who can change shared information

The Group Owner acts as the Group's Administrator and can edit or delete any Group record in any feature below, regardless of who created it. Administrative changes by the Owner do not change a record's original creator, Chef, Reader or Watching-with membership.

  • Calendar and Lists: every Member can see and edit Group records. Attendees and assignments describe who an item concerns; they do not limit access.
  • Recipes: all Members see Group Recipes; the creator can edit or delete their own Recipe, and the Group Owner can edit or delete any Group Recipe.
  • Watchlist: Members included in “Watching with” can update shared status, progress and source, and manage their own ratings. The creator or the Group Owner can delete the item; the Owner can also update any Group item without being included in Watching with.
  • Books: all Members see Group Books; each Reader manages their own Book records, and the Group Owner can edit or delete any Group Book.

Personal records are never part of Group administration — the Owner has no access to another Member's Personal Events, Lists or Reminders.

These are Planita app permissions. The underlying CloudKit share has broader access, so these are not separate server-enforced permissions for every record.

Syncing and working offline

Planita allows you to work with information already available on your device while offline. Changes normally synchronise through iCloud when connectivity returns. If several Members change the same information before synchronisation completes, some changes may be overwritten.
