4. Groups and Sharing

Planita allows users to create Groups for sharing information with family members, friends or other invited participants.

The person who creates a Group becomes its Owner.

The Owner is responsible for managing invitations and membership, including removing Members when necessary. The Owner also acts as the Group’s administrator and may amend or delete any information within the Group, regardless of who originally created it.

Group Members can access shared information according to the permissions available within Planita.

Some information may be editable by all Members, while other information may only be amended or deleted by its creator or, in their administrative capacity, the Group Owner.

When joining a Group, you acknowledge that:

  • Existing Group information may become visible to you.
  • Information you contribute may become visible to other Members.
  • Your contributions and associated historical records may remain in the Group after your membership ends, and may be amended or deleted by the Group Owner.
  • Previously accessed information may remain in screenshots, backups or other copies outside Planita’s control.

Members who wish to leave a Group should ask the Owner to remove them.

Group Owners should invite only people they trust and exercise appropriate care when managing shared information, including when exercising their administrative permissions.
