Get started

User Guide

Open a feature in Planita and tap its ? button for a quick reminder. Tap the Planita heading to open the Navigation Wheel and move between the six features.

On this pageHomeCalendarListsRecipesWatchlistBooksSharing

Home

Read the Home Guide →

Home brings together upcoming Events and outstanding dated Reminders, including those that are overdue. Use Filter to choose which items appear, including Group information where applicable. Open an item to view or edit it in its original feature.

Calendar

Read the Calendar Guide →

Add an event, set its date and time, and choose whether it repeats. When creating an event, choose Personal for your private calendar or Group to share it with active Group Members. Open an event to see its details and available actions.

Lists

Read the Lists Guide →

Create a list for a purpose, then add tasks and reminders. Mark items complete as you work through them. Reminders can repeat; future occurrences may be hidden until they approach their due date. Use List Options to show them.

Choose Personal or Group when creating a List. Members can collaborate on Group lists. Assigning a reminder helps organise work but does not make it private.

Recipes

Read the Recipes Guide →

Add ingredients, preparation instructions and an optional photograph. Search your collection to find a recipe. In an active Group, recipes are visible to every Member. The creator can edit or delete their recipe through Planita.

Watchlist

Read the Watchlist Guide →

Add films or television programmes and update viewing status. For television, track episodes as you watch. In a Group, Watchlist items are in the shared collection. Members included in “Watching with” can update shared viewing progress and manage their own ratings; the creator can delete the item.

Books

Read the Books Guide →

Add a book and record its details, reading progress and rating. In a Group, book records are visible to all Members. The person who created a book record can edit or delete it through Planita.

Personal and Group

For the full explanation of Group invitations, visibility, permissions and what happens when someone is removed, read Sharing & Groups.
